# Payam E Insaniyat Forum

**Based in Jhansi, India | Est. 2020 | Govt. Recognized & 80G Verified**

**Who We Are** Payam E Insaniyat (Message of Humanity) Forum is a registered NGO dedicated to uplifting marginalized, homeless, and underprivileged communities. We believe in transparency, direct action, and sustainable, ground-level impact.

**What We Do** We operate continuous community drives to address multi-dimensional poverty and human dignity:
* **Essential Needs:** Feeding the hungry through **Roti Bank** and clothing the poor via **Kapda Bank**.
* **Healthcare & Dignity:** Providing free medical camps, hospital support, and **Antim Yatra Sewa** (respectful, free funeral transportation).
* **Empowerment & Action:** Connecting youth to jobs through **Rojgar Bank**, funding urgent medical needs, and driving environmental and social activism.

**Our Mission** To ensure no call for help goes unanswered. 100% of public funds go directly toward our charitable initiatives. 

**Join Us** Whether you donate, volunteer your time, or amplify our message, your support brings immediate relief to those who need it most. *Be the reason someone smiles again.*
